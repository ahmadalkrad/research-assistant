Accelerate Your Academic Research with AI

research-assistant is an automated platform designed to simplify literature reviews. By taking raw data queries from users, our system leverages advanced AI agents to curate, summarize, and deliver the gist of the latest academic papers.

🔍

Intelligent Search

Submit raw data and search topics. Our AI engine actively scours academic databases to find the most relevant and recent research papers and articles.

🧠

AI Summarization

Complex papers are routed to our AI summarization agents. We extract the core methodologies, findings, and the "gist" of the research so you don't have to read hundreds of pages.

📑

Clear Citations

Receive perfectly structured outputs containing the exact names of the articles, their sources, and the concise AI-generated summaries ready for your review.
